Hotel Story

Built for the Two Horizons

Sri Krishna Grand Rooms and Hall began as a small family guesthouse a short walk from Kanyakumari Beach, at the one stretch of Indian coast where the sun both rises and sets over the sea. Two decades on, it has grown into a full-service hotel while keeping the same promise: a clean, comfortable room and a view worth waking up for.

Every room is planned around natural light and sea air, and every staff member grew up in the town — so recommendations for the best sunrise point or the quietest table at the fountain show come from people who actually go there.

Our Mission

Comfort First, Always

We exist to make a Kanyakumari trip effortless — a base close enough to walk to the shore, calm enough to actually rest, and priced fairly for families, solo travellers and business guests alike.

That means clear pricing, honest room descriptions, spotless bathrooms, and a front desk that answers the phone at 2 a.m. if a bus arrives late.
